## What makes a line work

A strong roast gives a specific, concrete scene, then lands on a vivid and surprising comparison. A first-person gripe makes it personal, and a tight two-beat structure keeps the punchline quick and well under the limit. Generic complaints and familiar angles like a dying phone battery, rating popups, or firmware updates are easy to forget.
